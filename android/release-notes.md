First Android release of SoilBoring: the same app as https://soilboring.vercel.app, installed on the phone.

- **Photos from the camera or the gallery** for logs, CPT, field worksheets and lab sheets. Camera photos are also kept in Pictures/SoilBoring.
- **GPS position** for boreholes and survey points. The location permission is asked the first time a page needs it.
- **Exports** (CSV, KML, AGS4, recovery codes) are saved to Download/SoilBoring and opened with a suitable app.
- **Print / save as PDF** opens the Android print dialog on A4.
- **Offline logging** works as in the browser: a log submitted without a connection stays on the phone and is sent when the connection is back.
- The back button goes back inside the app. A retry screen appears when there is no connection and nothing is stored yet.

Android 8.0 or newer. Install `soilboring-v1.0.0.apk` below, or add `https://github.com/Ariastiadi/SoilBoring` in Obtainium or Komi Store to get updates.
